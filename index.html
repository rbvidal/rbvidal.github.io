Knowledge Systems

Software can do more than automate tasks.

It can preserve, organize and operationalize knowledge. My work focuses on building systems that help organizations retrieve information, connect knowledge and support decision making.

Information Retrieval

Finding relevant information across large, heterogeneous document collections. Hybrid search combining lexical matching with semantic understanding. Vector search, BM25, reranking, and query-time filtering.

Knowledge Representation

Structuring information so that entities, relationships and context become explicit and queryable. Knowledge graphs, entity resolution, metadata extraction and graph-based reasoning.

Document Intelligence

Extracting structure and meaning from documents at scale. Parsing, classification, chunking strategies and embedding pipelines that prepare content for retrieval and reasoning.

Software Infrastructure

Building systems that keep sensitive information under organizational control. Local language model deployment, on-premise infrastructure, API design and system architecture.

About

Ralph Brandão Vidal

Berlin, Germany

Over twenty years of software engineering, with work spanning natural language processing, information retrieval, knowledge representation, enterprise software and machine learning.

My focus is building systems that help organizations preserve, organize and operationalize knowledge. This has led from early work in content management and enterprise portals through semantic search and knowledge graphs to the integration of local language models into knowledge systems. Artificial Intelligence is one component of these systems — not the purpose of the systems themselves.

Advanced studies (Hauptstudium) in Computer Science at Humboldt University of Berlin. M.Sc. in Geological Sciences (Geophysics), Freie Universität Berlin. Native Portuguese speaker. Fluent in German and English.
